Safety & app settingsRules, pain guidance, connection

Mission rules

  • Do not make up missed sessions. Continue with the plan.
  • Work crisis priority: long hike → one gym → second gym → conditioning → optional run.
  • Gym: stop most sets with 2–3 reps in reserve; no maxing, no routine failure training.
  • Pack: progress gradually toward 6–7 kg. Secure any kettlebells; water ballast is better.
  • Running: optional, easy, 0–1×/week. Reduce first if shin symptoms return.
  • Daily movement: ~8k easy steps is a baseline, not a punishment.
  • Nutrition: small surplus ~150–250 kcal/day; protein ~100–120 g/day; keep carbs adequate.
  • Taper: Dec 7–13 is deliberately easy. No hero workouts.

Pain rules

0–2/10: continue and monitor.
3–4/10: do not increase load; reduce the provoking activity if needed.
5+/10 or sharp/localized pain: stop/reduce the provoking activity.

Persistent or worsening shin, Achilles, knee, foot, chest, breathing, neurological or other concerning symptoms need proper medical assessment. This app is training guidance, not diagnosis.

  • 6–7 kg pack
  • Then 3–4 h easy hiking the next day
